Nexa Remote for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Local TV control

Nexa Remote discovers and controls compatible Android TV and Google TV devices on your local network. The remote does not require an account or a cloud relay. TV addresses, pairing credentials, saved devices, and preferences are stored on your phone. Remote commands and text are sent directly to the selected TV.

Ads and purchases

Android build 3 (1.0.0) has no ads or subscriptions. Build 4 (1.1.0) includes Google Mobile Ads, its consent SDK, and Google Play Billing. Banner ads may appear on the Devices, Saved, and Settings screens, but not on the pairing or remote screens. Ads are requested only when the consent SDK permits them. Where required, the app presents consent choices and an option to revisit them.

Google Mobile Ads automatically collects and shares IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interaction information, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that these data are encrypted in transit. See Google's data disclosure for details. Nexa Remote does not receive this advertising data on its own server.

If a monthly ad-removal subscription is offered, Google Play processes the purchase. The app reads purchase status and a purchase token through Google Play Billing to remove banners; it does not receive payment card details or send purchase data to a Nexa Remote server.

Voice and permissions

If you choose voice control, the app requests microphone access and streams the voice command directly to the connected TV over the local network. The optional phone speech-to-text action invokes the speech recognition service installed on your device; that provider may process audio under its own privacy policy. Bluetooth permissions support direct control on compatible devices. You can deny or revoke optional permissions in Android Settings.

Retention and control

Saved TVs and preferences remain on your phone until you remove them or uninstall the app. You can remove a saved TV in the app. Google ad and purchase data are managed through Google's privacy and Play controls. Nexa Remote has no user account to delete.

Children

Nexa Remote is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date. For privacy or support questions, open a support request.
