Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

Overview

Nexa Remote controls supported Android TV and Google TV devices directly over your local Wi-Fi network. The app does not require an account or a cloud relay.

Data collection

Nexa Remote does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data. The iPhone app does not include advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or third-party tracking.

Local network access

The app uses local network access to discover compatible TVs, establish a secure connection, and send remote-control commands. This traffic stays between your iPhone and TV on the local network.

Data stored on your device

Paired-device details, connection preferences, language, appearance, and remote-layout settings are stored locally on your iPhone. You can remove saved TVs in the app or remove all local app data by uninstalling Nexa Remote.

Permissions

  • Local Network: discovers and connects to compatible TVs.
  • Bonjour: locates Android TV, Google TV, and Cast services on the same network.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
